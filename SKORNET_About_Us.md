# SKORNET

## About Us

## Connecting the HVAC Service Ecosystem

SKORNET is building a digital platform designed to connect the people, processes and operations that power the HVAC service industry. Our approach brings service demand, business operations, field activities and customer service delivery into one connected ecosystem.

## Bringing Every Stage of Service Together

HVAC service operations often involve multiple stakeholders, communication channels and manual processes. SKORNET aims to bring these activities into a more structured digital workflow—from managing service requests and assigning work to coordinating appointments, tracking field progress and maintaining service records.

By bringing essential operational information together, the platform helps service businesses gain better visibility into day-to-day activities and coordinate work across their operations.

## Connecting Businesses, Field Teams and Customers

SKORNET is designed to support the connection between service companies, their workforce and customer service delivery. With capabilities for service-call management, workforce coordination, digital records, attendance and field activity tracking, the platform helps organise information that businesses need to manage their service operations.

## Turning Operations into Useful Insights

Beyond coordinating daily activities, SKORNET aims to make operational information more accessible and useful. Structured service records and field activity data can help businesses review workflows, understand service progress and make more informed operational decisions.

Our focus is to simplify complex workflows, improve coordination between stakeholders and support a more connected, transparent and organised HVAC service ecosystem.
