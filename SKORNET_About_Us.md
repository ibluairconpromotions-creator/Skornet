### Overview
SKORNET is a digital platform designed to connect the people, processes and operations that power the HVAC service industry across India. From technician verification and service-call assignment to attendance tracking and digital work history, SKORNET helps organize the information and workflows behind HVAC service delivery.

### The Industry Challenge
HVAC service operations manage a distributed technician workforce, but day-to-day coordination is often fragmented across personal WhatsApp groups, phone calls, paper job sheets, and disconnected spreadsheets. This creates manual administrative friction, limited field visibility, verification gaps, and attendance tracking disputes.

### One Connected Platform. Multiple Stakeholders.
SKORNET brings together the four key participants in the HVAC service lifecycle:
1. **Service Business Owners:** Workforce visibility, service-call dispatch and monitoring, attendance tracking, and approvals for overtime, expenses, and incentives.
2. **Field Technicians:** Verified professional digital identity, assigned service calls, appointment reminders, and digital work history via the technician mobile application.
3. **Platform Administrators:** Centralized technician verification, KYC documentation checks, and organizational association governance.
4. **Customers & Facility Teams:** Punctual appointment coordination, verified skilled technician dispatch, and structured digital service records.

### Operational Workflow: From Verification to Completion
1. **Verify Technician Records:** Authenticate KYC, Aadhaar, and specialized skill matrices.
2. **Assign Service Calls:** Systematically match service requests to technician capability and location.
3. **Coordinate Appointments:** Deliver schedule reminders and controlled customer contact links.
4. **Track GPS Field Activity:** Validate daily attendance and record travel distance in kilometers.
5. **Record Service Progress:** Document diagnostic checklists, maintenance notes, and customer sign-offs.

### Supported Equipment Categories
- Water-cooled and air-cooled Chillers
- Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF/VRV) systems
- Air Handling Units (AHUs)
- Fan Coil Units (FCUs)
- Rooftop Packaged Units
- Cooling Towers
- Ventilation & Exhaust Systems
- Precision Air Conditioning (PAC) Units

### Data Access and Trust
- **Organizational Isolation:** Technician details and customer accounts are private to each owner organization and never shared with competitors.
- **Confidential Identity Protection:** Aadhaar and identity files are stored confidentially for authorized compliance.
- **Time-Limited Links:** Customer contact links expire automatically after call completion.
